WALL-E and EVE's Signal

An autonomous agent searches a castle it has never seen for a lost princess. It has one noisy distress call, four sensors, a battery, and a target who is running out of energy. The belief

Teal glow: where the agent thinks the princess is, brighter = more likely. Solid outline: the smallest area holding 50% of that probability; dashed: 90%. The diamond marks the single most likely cell.

Sensors

Each scan sends out a ring to its range: thermal (heat, 50 m), radar (heartbeats through walls, 20 m), LIDAR (walls and shapes, 10 m), camera (4 cells ahead, gives a label).

Decoys

Braziers and hearths are hot, dogs have heartbeats, suits of armour are person-shaped. Only the full signature (warm, heartbeat, human shape and a camera target label) convinces the agent.

Battery

Every action costs battery. A safety rule keeps enough charge to walk home to a hearth; near the end an endgame override weighs going home against pushing on while she is still alive.
